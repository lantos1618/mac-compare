How to read the table

Each merged box applies to the rows it spans. Performance is the candidate’s Geekbench 7 score divided by your selected Mac’s score. A 2.00× result means twice the benchmark score. GPU OpenCL measures compute. Game frame rates depend on the game and settings.

Each price links to its US source for that configuration. Current price is the lowest recorded US price across new listings, refurbished listings and completed used sales. The condition and source appear below the amount. Price when new is the historical MSRP for that configuration. New retail uses recorded new listings. Refurbished prices include the seller or condition grade. A Used price is an individual completed sale on Swappa, with the condition and sale month shown. A dash means no US price was verified for that condition. An external screen identifies a Mac Studio. PC match percentages compare the PC component with the chip on that row.

Each dot is a benchmark score from the table. Each family’s line connects its highest score at each x value. The ring marks your Mac. Memory points reuse chip benchmarks across listed capacities.
